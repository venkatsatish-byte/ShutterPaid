Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date]

This policy explains what ShutterPaid collects, why, and the choices you have. ShutterPaid is run by [company legal name], 405 Cameron Bridge Way, Johns Creek, GA 30022, USA ("we", "us").

The short version

  • You can use ShutterPaid without an account. Your data then stays in your browser on your device and we never receive it.
  • If you create an account, we store your invoices, settings and reminder history so they sync between your devices and so automatic reminders can be sent.
  • We don't sell your data or your clients' data, and we don't use it for advertising.
  • Our analytics don't use cookies and don't identify you.

What we collect

If you use ShutterPaid without an account

Invoices and settings are stored only in your browser's local storage. They are not sent to us. Clearing your browser data deletes them, so export a backup from Settings if you want to keep a copy.

If you create an account

  • Account details: your email address, and your name and profile picture if you sign in with Google.
  • Your settings: your name, business name, currency, payment link, email signature, reminder schedule, time zone and reply-to address.
  • Invoices you add: client name and email, shoot type, invoice number, amount, dates, notes and whether it has been paid.
  • Reminder history: when each reminder was sent or copied, its tone, and for automatic reminders the recipient address and delivery status.
  • Billing: if you subscribe to Pro, your plan and subscription status. Card details are handled by Stripe and never reach our servers.
  • Online payments from your clients: if you connect your own Stripe account, we store its ID, whether it can accept payments, and the payment link for each invoice. Your clients pay you through Stripe; their card details go to Stripe, not to us. When a client pays, Stripe tells us so we can mark the invoice paid.

Joining the beta list

If you join the beta list on our website, we store the email address you give us and the date. We use it only to send you news about ShutterPaid; you can ask us to remove it at any time.

Website analytics

We use Plausible Analytics to count visits and a few actions (such as sign-ups and upgrades). Plausible doesn't use cookies, doesn't collect personal data and doesn't track you across websites.

Your clients' data

When you add a client to ShutterPaid, you decide what is stored and whether they receive reminders. For that data you are the controller and we act as your processor: we store it and send reminders on your instructions, and use it for nothing else. You are responsible for having a lawful basis to contact your clients about their invoices.

Every automatic reminder names your business and includes a link that lets the client stop reminders for that invoice. We honour that choice immediately.

How we use data

  • To provide the service: sync your data, send the reminders you set up, and show your reminder history.
  • To run billing for Pro subscriptions.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.
  • To reply when you contact us.

How long we keep data

We keep your account data while your account is open. When you erase your data in Settings, your invoices, reminder history and settings are deleted from our database. Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, or object to how we use it. You can export your data yourself from Settings at any time. For anything else, email us. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Security
